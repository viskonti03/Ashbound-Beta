# Bankai sprite assets

AI-generated full-body transparent PNG illustrations for Ashbound, based on the character designs supplied in this conversation. Front assets are in this folder; matching rear assets are in back/.

Characters: dan, afrodi, blade, drake, gin, kami, kidou, lane, tomamaru.

Art direction: preserve each character's identity, hair, robes and power theme; depict the awakened Bankai form as a complete standing battle sprite with visible feet, generous edge clearance and transparent background. Rear variants use the corresponding generated front as visual reference and turn the same form away from the camera, retaining matching clothing and power effects. Tomamaru's awakened form uses lightning without a weapon.

The runtime selects the image from the actual Bankai state and camera side. These files contain artwork only; gameplay remains in src/game.
